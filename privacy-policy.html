2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to visitors, candidates, employer representatives and people who contact Rolexa or are included in platform content. It covers the Rolexa website, account journeys, dashboards, hiring workflows, Career Passport features, support and related communications.

Employers are separate controllers for their own recruitment decisions and use of candidate information. Their privacy notices also apply. This policy does not control third-party websites reached through external links.

3. Personal information we collect

  • Account and identity data: name, email, user ID, account type, authentication provider, account status and security events.
  • Candidate data: contact details, location, work history, education, skills, salary expectations, preferences, CVs, professional links, profile images and career-direction information.
  • UK work-eligibility declarations: whether a candidate says they currently have permission to work in the UK, whether hours or types of work are restricted, whether that permission expires, the stated expiry date where applicable, sponsorship requirements, declaration status and timestamps. Rolexa does not request a passport, share code or identity document during account registration.
  • Hiring activity: saved jobs, applications, stages, interview details, offers, outcomes, messages, notifications, employer notes where appropriate and activity timestamps.
  • Career Passport data: evidence, verification status, provenance, permissions, access grants and revocations.
  • Employer and company data: representative details, job title, work email, organisation profile, company number, registered office, website and company-verification submissions and outcomes.
  • Jobs and platform content: job descriptions, requirements, salary, location, work style, questions, support requests, feedback and reported concerns.
  • Technical and audit data: IP address where available, browser and device details, logs, session information, security signals, consent version, acceptance time and related audit records.
  • Communications: messages sent through Rolexa and correspondence with support or legal contacts.

4. Where information comes from

We collect information directly from users, from activity on the platform, from authorised colleagues acting for an employer, and from service providers supporting authentication, hosting, communications, analytics or security. We may receive publicly available company information from sources such as Companies House or an employer's website when verifying a hiring organisation.

If another user provides information about you, they must have authority and a lawful basis. You should not provide excessive or irrelevant information about other people.

6. Recruitment, sensitive information and employer access

Candidate profiles and applications may be made available to relevant verified employer users as part of a genuine hiring process. Career Passport evidence is shared only according to the candidate's platform permissions, subject to essential security and legal access.

The UK work-eligibility answers collected during registration are self-declared and remain marked as unverified. They may be used to identify whether a role appears compatible with stated work conditions or whether clarification is required. They do not replace an employer's official right-to-work check, and candidates must be given a fair opportunity to establish their eligibility through an appropriate lawful route before employment.

Do not upload special-category information (such as health, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation or trade-union membership) or criminal-conviction information unless Rolexa provides a specific lawful field and explains why it is required. Where sensitive information is genuinely processed, Rolexa and the relevant employer must identify an Article 9 condition or other additional legal basis.

Employers must use candidate data only for lawful recruitment and apply their own retention, access and fairness controls.

  • courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other recipients where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety; and

10. Security

Rolexa uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, row-level database controls, restricted permissions, encrypted network connections, audit records and separation of candidate sharing and employer verification controls.

No system is completely secure. Users must protect their login details and report suspected compromise. Rolexa will assess personal-data breaches and notify affected people and the Information Commissioner's Office where legally required.

11. Matching and automated tools

Rolexa may use rules, scoring or automated tools to rank, organise or recommend jobs and candidates, estimate apparent fit, generate guidance and identify unusual activity. These outputs support users and are not intended to make solely automated employment decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

Employers remain responsible for meaningful human review and fair hiring decisions. You may ask for information about a relevant automated process, challenge inaccurate personal information or contact us if you believe an output has unfairly affected you.
